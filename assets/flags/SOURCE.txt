Country flags from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org), public domain or CC0 files only.
Fetched 2026-10-25: each code's country by ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 on Wikidata (P297) -> its flag image (P41) ->
the Commons file, its licence read from Commons (extmetadata LicenseShortName); codes without an ISO entry
were looked up by name. Files are as downloaded except the <!DOCTYPE> line, removed where there was one.
Many files are tagged "insignia" on Commons: official symbols, free of copyright but some uses of them can
be restricted by other laws.
Niger (ne) is left out (Roblox moderation, see the project memory); "uk" is not a separate flag (it would
duplicate "gb"). Regional flag: california (added 2026-09-29).
